# SFX catalog (gợi ý theo event)

## UI

- `ui_click`: 30–80ms, pitch cao, decay nhanh
- `ui_confirm`: 80–150ms, 2 nốt lên (ok)
- `ui_cancel`: 80–150ms, 2 nốt xuống (no)

## Movement

- `jump`: 120–220ms, pitch lên nhanh (attack) rồi rơi nhẹ
- `dash`: 150–300ms, noise “whoosh” + pitch xuống
- `land`: 50–120ms, low thump (noise + low tone)

## Combat

- `hit_light`: 60–120ms, clicky + chút noise
- `hit_heavy`: 120–240ms, low + noise mạnh
- `enemy_die`: 200–500ms, pitch xuống + noise fade

## Rewards

- `pickup_coin`: 80–150ms, arpeggio 2–3 nốt lên
- `pickup_item`: 150–300ms, “sparkle” (high + quick repeats)

## Environment

- `door_open`: 200–400ms, pitch lên + click
- `switch_toggle`: 50–120ms, 1 click rõ
